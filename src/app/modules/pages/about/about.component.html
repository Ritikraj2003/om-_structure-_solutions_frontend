OUR STORY

A Decade of Engineering
Excellence

OM Enterprises was founded in 2011 as a proprietorship firm by Mr. Dhirendra Singh. With steady growth and excellence in delivering structural solutions, the company was built on a foundation of integrity and technical precision.

Over the years, we expanded our footprint across India, undertaking complex industrial and commercial projects. Our commitment to quality led to significant growth, and in 2020, the firm was converted into OM Structures Solution (India) Ltd, marking a milestone of ten years of dedicated service to the construction industry.
